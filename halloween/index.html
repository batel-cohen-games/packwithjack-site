Halloween with Jack

Jack dresses up as a little vampire and the whole game turns spooky-sweet - and the Daily Challenge becomes a Halloween countdown, with a new spooky case to pack every day.

Halloween update

The Halloween Daily countdown

From October 25 the Daily Challenge gets a new Halloween case every day: seven designs counting down to the monster on Halloween night, then two encores to November 2. The same timed puzzle, two tries and a trophy for every win.

A new look everywhere

The loading screen, Jack on his Halloween doorstep, his first suitcase and a Halloween app icon.

Seven spooky cases

A new Halloween case for the Daily Challenge every day until Halloween night: pumpkin, coffin, cauldron, haunted house, candy box, mummy and the monster.

Same cozy puzzles

Every level, the journal and your progress stay as they are.
