Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

Who we are

Pack With Jack is an iPhone game provided by Batel Cohen, an independent developer in Israel. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

Contact: support@packwithjack.com. Replies may come from packwithjackgame@gmail.com.

The game and your progress

You do not need an account to play, and there is no sign-in. The game has no in-app purchases. It includes advertising (see below). The coins you earn are spent only inside the game.

Your completed levels, journal, Daily Challenge record, coins and Jack's Home are saved on your device and synced through your own iCloud using Apple's key-value storage, so your progress can follow you to another iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud on. We cannot read your iCloud game data. Apple operates iCloud under its own privacy terms.

Optional analytics and advertising measurement

When you choose ALLOW DATA in the first-launch window, we use Unity Analytics and AppsFlyer to understand how the game is played, to improve it, and to measure which of our ads bring players to the game. Choose NO THANKS to keep them off; you can still play. You can change your choice at any time with SHARE DATA in Settings. Turning it off stops future collection by these two services; it does not by itself delete what was already sent.

These services may receive installation and device identifiers (such as the IDFV, an AppsFlyer installation ID and a Unity player ID); technical information such as device model, iOS version, language and app version; your IP address and the approximate country or region derived from it; and information about how the game is played: level starts, completions, restarts and exits, hints, Daily Challenge attempts and results, the first-launch steps, and visits and purchases in Jack's Home. "Purchases" here means spending in-game coins, never real money. AppsFlyer also receives the campaign that brought you to the game, game launches, and, when you have allowed data sharing, events for completed rewarded videos and full-screen ads (with the placement or level). It may receive launch time and battery level to help detect advertising fraud.

Our legal basis for this optional processing is your consent. Unity and AppsFlyer process this information for us as service providers, as described in Unity's player privacy policy and AppsFlyer's Services Privacy Policy.

We advertise the game on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), and AppsFlyer is connected to Meta Ads. For players who chose ALLOW DATA, AppsFlyer may tell Meta about the install and the in-game events above so Meta can measure our campaigns. Meta processes this under its own privacy policy. Players who chose NO THANKS are not reported to Meta by AppsFlyer.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to show ads:

  • HINT: HINT is free. While it rests for two minutes after a hint, you may choose to watch a video to get a hint at once.
  • DOUBLE IT!: after the first PERFECT PACK of a level, you may choose to watch a video to double that level's coins.
  • Between levels: a full-screen ad may appear when you tap NEXT LEVEL, from level 11, at a limited pace.

The game never interrupts a level you are playing with an ad. There are no ads in the Daily Challenge or in Jack's Home.

To deliver and measure ads, keep its services working and prevent fraud, Google may process your IP address and approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, information about the ads you see and interact with, and diagnostic and performance information. What is available depends on your device settings and your choices. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Personalized ads may use information about your interests and activity. Non-personalized ads do not use your past behavior to choose the ad, but they still use technical information, for example to limit how often you see an ad and to prevent fraud. If you choose NO THANKS (or turn SHARE DATA off), the game asks Google for non-personalized ads only. Declining does not remove ads from the game.

We ask Google only for ads rated G (suitable for general audiences), and we block sensitive ad categories such as gambling, alcohol and dating. These settings cannot guarantee that every ad is suitable for everyone: if you see an ad that you think is inappropriate, use Report an Ad in Settings.

Your choices

  • ALLOW DATA / NO THANKS and SHARE DATA control Unity Analytics and AppsFlyer, and whether ads may be personalized.
  • Apple's tracking permission: if you have chosen ALLOW DATA, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt may appear after you finish level 2. The IDFA (Apple's advertising identifier) is available only if you allow tracking there; you can decline and still play. You can change it at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. SHARE DATA does not change this Apple setting. While Apple's permission is unanswered, AppsFlyer waits up to five minutes before reporting the install; after that, or if you decline, it reports without the IDFA. Apple's SKAdNetwork may give ad networks aggregated install reports that do not identify you.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your advertising choices after level 2, and Ad Privacy in Settings lets you review them later.

When you contact us or report an ad

We receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. The CONTACT US and Report an Ad drafts include the app version, iPhone model and iOS version to help us look into the problem; a Report an Ad draft also names the last ad shown (which, when, and Google's ID for it) so the ad can be found. You can review or remove any of it before sending. A deletion draft includes your AppsFlyer ID when one exists on your device, so we can locate the relevant record. We use this correspondence only to answer you and handle your requests, relying on our legitimate interest in supporting players and, for privacy requests, our legal obligations. Email to our support address is forwarded by our domain provider (Squarespace) to a Google Gmail inbox.

Information we do not request

The game does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, health information or payment details.

How long information is kept

Unity Analytics keeps personal data for 13 months by default (see Unity's privacy overview). AppsFlyer keeps the data it processes for us for no more than 24 months, unless we ask for earlier deletion or the law requires otherwise (see its Services Privacy Policy). Google keeps advertising data according to Google's retention policy.

We keep support emails for up to 12 months after your request is resolved, and delete them sooner when they are no longer needed. We keep them longer only if the law requires it or to handle a dispute.

Your game progress stays under your control on your device and in your Apple account. Deleting the app removes the progress stored on your iPhone, but a copy saved in your own iCloud can come back if you reinstall with the same Apple ID; we can't access or delete that copy. Contact us if you need help.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR or UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information for money. Personalized advertising through Google may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some US state laws. You can stop it at any time: choose NO THANKS or turn SHARE DATA off (the game then asks only for non-personalized ads), and decline tracking in Apple's prompt or in iPhone Settings. Where such laws apply, you also have the right to know, correct and delete your personal information, without being treated differently for using these rights.

DELETE MY DATA in Settings turns SHARE DATA off, sends Unity a request to delete your Unity Analytics data, and prepares an email to us so we can ask AppsFlyer to delete its record (with your AppsFlyer ID when available). Opening the draft does not send it: you need to send the email. It does not change Apple's tracking permission or your Google advertising choices, and it does not erase your game progress in iCloud. You can also write to support@packwithjack.com. We may ask for limited information to locate your record, and we will respond within the time the law requires.

Children

Pack With Jack is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it once identified.

International processing

Google, Unity, AppsFlyer, Meta and their service providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area and the UK, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in their policies.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and change the effective date above. Where required, we will tell you in the game and ask for your consent again before any materially different processing.
