Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Who we are

Pack With Jack is an iPhone game provided by Batel Cohen, an individual developer in Israel. We are responsible for the personal data described here.

Contact: support@packwithjack.com. Replies may come from packwithjackgame@gmail.com.

Your choice

You can play without agreeing to optional analytics. AppsFlyer, our advertising measurement and analytics provider, starts only after you choose ALLOW DATA. Choose NO THANKS to keep it off. You can withdraw this consent at any time in Settings by turning SHARE DATA off. This stops future optional collection; it does not by itself delete information already sent. To request deletion, use DELETE MY DATA in Settings or email us.

Optional advertising measurement and analytics

With your consent, AppsFlyer receives device identifiers, including the IDFV and an AppsFlyer installation identifier; your IP address and the approximate country or region derived from it; device model, iOS version, language, locale and app version; and install time and campaign attribution information. The IDFA (Apple's advertising identifier) is available only if you separately allow tracking through Apple's permission prompt.

We send these milestones once per player: finishing the tutorial, first completing each level (its number), completing each world (its name), and winning a Daily Challenge for the first time. AppsFlyer also measures game launches and may receive launch time and battery level to help detect advertising fraud.

We use this information to measure which ads bring players to the game and to understand progress so we can improve the game. Our legal basis for this optional processing is your consent. AppsFlyer processes this information for us as our service provider, as described in its Services Privacy Policy.

We have not connected any advertising networks to AppsFlyer. If we start advertising campaigns, AppsFlyer may share install and in-game event information with the advertising networks we use so they can measure those campaigns. We will update this policy before that happens.

Apple's tracking permission

If you have agreed to data sharing, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt may appear after you finish level 2. Allowing data sharing is not the same as allowing tracking: you can decline tracking and still play. You can change this permission at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

While Apple's permission is unanswered, AppsFlyer waits up to five minutes from its start before reporting the install; after that, or if you decline, it reports without the IDFA. Without your permission we do not access your IDFA and do not use your data to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Apple's SKAdNetwork may provide aggregated ad-install reports to ad networks; these reports do not give us personal information about you.

Progress and iCloud

Your completed levels, journal state and Daily Challenge record are saved on your device and synced through your own iCloud using Apple's key-value storage, so your progress can follow you to another iPhone signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud on. We cannot read your iCloud game data. There is no game account or sign-in. Apple operates iCloud under its own privacy terms.

When you contact us

We receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. The CONTACT US draft includes the app version, iPhone model and iOS version to help diagnose problems; you can remove them before sending. A deletion draft includes your AppsFlyer ID when one exists on your device, so we can locate the relevant record. We use support correspondence only to respond and to handle your requests, relying on our legitimate interest in supporting players and, for privacy requests, our legal obligations. Email to our support address is forwarded by our domain provider (Squarespace) to a Google Gmail inbox.

Unity and information we do not request

The game is built with Unity. Unity Analytics and Unity's other online services are turned off in this game.

The game does not ask for your name, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, health information or payment details. Version 1.0 has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no in-game advertising.

How long information is kept

AppsFlyer keeps the data it processes for us according to its policy: generally up to 24 months, unless we ask for earlier deletion or the law requires otherwise. See AppsFlyer's retention guidance.

We keep support emails for up to 12 months after your request is resolved, and delete them sooner when they are no longer needed. We keep them longer only if the law requires it or to handle a dispute.

Your game progress stays under your control on your device and in your Apple account. Deleting the app removes the progress stored on your iPhone, but a copy saved in your own iCloud can come back if you reinstall with the same Apple ID; we can't access or delete that copy. Contact us if you need help.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR or UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information. Where California law applies, you have the right to know, correct and delete your personal information and to opt out of its sale or sharing, without being treated differently for using these rights. Turning SHARE DATA off stops future sharing for advertising measurement.

To make a request, use DELETE MY DATA in Settings or write to support@packwithjack.com. Opening the draft does not send it: you need to send the email. We may ask for limited information to locate your record, and we will respond within the time the law requires. A request to delete AppsFlyer data does not erase your game progress in iCloud.

Children

Pack With Jack is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it once identified.

International processing

AppsFlyer and its service providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area and the UK, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in AppsFlyer's policy.

Changes

We will update this policy when our practices change and change the effective date above. Where required, we will tell you in the game and ask for your consent again before any materially different optional processing.
