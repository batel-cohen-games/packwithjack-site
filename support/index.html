Support

Need a hand? Email support@packwithjack.com. Replies may arrive from packwithjackgame@gmail.com.

How do I play?

Drag the items into the container and tap an item to turn it. Fit every item with no gaps to complete the level. HOW TO PLAY in Settings shows the rules again.

What does HINT do, and why is there a wait?

HINT shows where one item goes. After using it, wait two minutes before the next hint; the button shows the time remaining. START OVER resets the current level if you want a fresh start.

What is the Daily Challenge?

It unlocks after level 5. Solve the day's packing puzzle before time runs out to earn a trophy. You have two tries each day. Complete Day 6 to unlock your gift.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID and keep iCloud on. The game syncs your progress through your own iCloud, with no separate game login. Allow some time and an internet connection for syncing. If your progress does not appear, contact us before deleting anything.

How do I turn data sharing or tracking off?

Turn SHARE DATA off in the game's Settings to stop optional AppsFlyer collection. Apple's separate tracking permission is in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Declining either one does not stop you from playing.

How do I delete my data?

Choose DELETE MY DATA in Settings, then send the prepared email. It includes your AppsFlyer ID when one exists on your device, to help us locate the record. You can also email us directly. We will confirm by email. This is a request, not an instant deletion button, and it does not clear your game progress on the device or in iCloud. If no mail app is set up on your iPhone, write to support@packwithjack.com.

Sound and vibration

SOUND turns music, sound effects and Jack's voice on or off. VIBRATION controls haptic feedback.
